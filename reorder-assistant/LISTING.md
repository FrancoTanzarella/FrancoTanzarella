## App name
Reorder Assistant

## Tagline / introduction
Know what to reorder in 2 minutes a week. Reorder suggestions and supplier POs for small stores.

## App details

Shopify retired Stocky. Most replacements are full inventory systems. Reorder Assistant does one job: it tells you what to reorder this week, and how much.

It looks at your stock on hand, stock already on the way and your recent sales, then gives you a reorder list grouped by supplier. Download a purchase order for each supplier as a CSV and send it.

- Reorder suggestions based on the last 60 days of sales
- Counts incoming stock, so you don't order twice
- Suppliers come from your product Vendor field. Set a lead time for each one
- Override any product with a fixed min/max or a case pack, or exclude it
- One-click CSV purchase order per supplier, with unit and cost totals
- No customer data stored

Built for small stores with up to 5,000 variants. Flat pricing, no surprises, and every support email answered within 24 hours.

## Testing instructions for the reviewer
1. Install the app and pick a plan when Shopify asks (both plans have a free trial).
2. On the Reorder page, click **Run first sync**. It takes up to a minute; the page refreshes by itself.
3. Products with low stock relative to recent sales appear under their vendor. Click **Download PO (CSV)** on a supplier.
4. Open **Suppliers** to change a lead time, then return to **Reorder** to see suggestions update.
5. The development store needs products with tracked inventory and some orders in the last 60 days for suggestions to appear.
